冷水魚智慧養殖系統

Cold Water Aquaculture System for Cherry Salmon · Oncorhynchus masou

本系統為櫻鱒（Cherry Salmon）冷水循環式養殖之科學監控與實驗平台。 以多階段水溫適應流程，從孵化、稚魚、中型適應、至大型育成池， 透過 IoT 感測即時掌握水溫、溶氧、pH 與斃死率， 並長期記錄成長與飼料效率作為實驗數據。

Multi-stage RAS for cold-water cherry salmon. Real-time IoT telemetry of temperature, DO, pH, NH₃/NO₂ across incubation → nursery → juvenile → grow-out, with long-term growth and FCR data captured as experimental record.
